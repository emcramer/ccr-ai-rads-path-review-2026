MEASURED REPAIR
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Dropping `predict\w+` from include[5]'s verb list, keeping generat/synthes/impute:
  63 of 65  =  97%   removes all 11 status-prediction false positives
                     and loses ZERO genuine papers.
Two alternatives were measured and are worse: requiring a stain/image noun costs
3 genuine papers for the same benefit.
Caveat: measured within the 76 the current patterns select. It cannot measure a
paper outside the row that uses "predict" generatively and is caught by nothing
else.
